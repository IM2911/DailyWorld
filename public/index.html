The cost of a lamp left on

A 60 W bulb burning day and night uses 525.6 kWh a year: $78.84 at $0.15 per kWh.

Off at night and on 8 hours a day, the same bulb comes to $26.28 a year.

Questions

About the figures, the sums and what happens to what you type.

Send us a question
Price per kWh 15$ Enter a valid price (e.g. 0.15)

Is DailyWorld free?

Yes. Every calculator and guide is free to use, with no account and no sign-up.

Why is a price of 15 flagged?

Prices are in dollars per kWh, so 15 cents is 0.15. A price over $1 is flagged in case cents were typed as dollars.

How exact is each cost estimate?

It is an estimate from rated watts and the hours you enter. A plug-in meter or your utility's reading gives the measured figure.

Where do each calculator's starting figures come from?

Each opens with a typical power and usage for that appliance and $0.15 per kWh, about the US average. Replace them with yours.

Which currency do the calculators use?

Results are shown in US dollars. The sums work for any price per kWh you type.

Does DailyWorld store what I type?

No. The calculators run in your browser, and the numbers you enter are not sent to us.
